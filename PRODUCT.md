# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Aniket Bamotra as a UX Engineer. They skim quickly, want to see real shipped work, and judge both design sensibility and front-end build quality from the site itself.

## Product Purpose
Single-page personal portfolio at aneeek.com: introduce Aniket, show selected projects with links to the live work, and make getting in touch easy. Success is a recruiter understanding what Aniket builds and reaching out.

## Positioning
A UX Engineer who both designs and builds; the portfolio itself is the proof of craft.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- React + Vite, plain CSS (`src/components/comp.css`, `globalStyle.css`), no UI library.
- Sections: TopMenu, Bio, Projects, Contact, footer. Projects are edited in the `projects` array in `src/components/Projects.jsx`.
- Hagrid font is used under a CC BY-NC trial license (non-commercial).

## Evidence on Hand
- Six projects with screenshots in `src/assets/images/`: Immersive Bionic (Behance case), Suncare Formulations, Purebet, Keploy, GEMx, Demand Climate Justice — each with a live URL.
- Project role and year are not yet supplied; do not invent them. No testimonials, metrics, or client quotes exist.

## Product Principles
- The site's own craft is the argument; every section should demonstrate design-and-build skill.
- Make the work scannable in seconds for a recruiter.
- Never fabricate roles, dates, or outcomes.
